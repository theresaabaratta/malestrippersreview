The content
What Kind of Entertainment?

We cover two main categories of male performance — and they're more distinct than people think.

Male Entertainers

Private event and party performers who bring high-energy shows directly to bachelorette parties, birthday events, and girls' nights. Think choreographed routines, crowd interaction, and unforgettable moments — all in your venue of choice.

Burlesque Performers

Stage-based artistic performance rooted in theatricality, comedy, and striptease tradition. Male burlesque is a growing art form — equal parts performance and personality. Great for cabaret nights, mixers, and venue shows.

Both are adult entertainment for consenting adult audiences. Both deserve to be reviewed and talked about openly — which is exactly what this site is for.

Common questions
Quick Answers
Can I book a performer through this site?

No — we are not a booking platform. We link to and list performer information for discovery purposes only. For bookings, contact the performer directly through their own channels.

Can performers submit their own profiles?

Yes. Performers can submit a profile to be listed on the site. Listings are free and editorial — we do not charge for placement and we do not accept payment in exchange for favorable coverage.

Are reviews moderated?

Yes. We review submissions for spam, fake reviews, and content that violates our guidelines before publishing. We don't remove negative reviews simply because a performer requests it.

Is this site appropriate for all audiences?

This site covers adult entertainment and is intended for audiences 18 and older. Content is informational and editorial — no explicit media is hosted here.

How do I get my city added?

Use the "Add a City" feature on the main site or contact us directly. We're actively expanding coverage and welcome suggestions for new markets.
